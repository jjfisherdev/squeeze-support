Squeeze — Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

The short version

Squeeze collects no data. None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no networking code — nothing you do in Squeeze ever leaves your device.

Data collection

Squeeze does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. The app makes no network connections of any kind.

Location (“Near You” mode)

If you choose the optional Near You mode, Squeeze asks for while-using location permission so it can pick trivia questions about places around you. Your coordinates are compared against a places database that ships inside the app, on your device. Your location is never transmitted, stored beyond the moment of the lookup, or shared with anyone — including us. Declining or revoking permission simply switches the app to Surprise mode.

Data stored on your device

Squeeze keeps a small amount of data locally on your iPhone so the game works: match-in-progress state, which questions you've already seen, your chosen hero avatars, and settings like the announcer toggle. This data stays on your device, is not backed up by us anywhere, and is removed entirely when you delete the app.

Third parties

Squeeze contains no third-party SDKs, services, or content.

Children

Squeeze is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

What is Squeeze?

A head-to-head estimation duel for two players sharing one iPhone or iPad. A real question appears and you bet how close your guess will land (“±300 feet”). Your rival squeezes that bet tighter, or calls your bluff: then you place your window on the bridge, and they call the answer BELOW or ABOVE it. If the real answer lands inside your window, you win the round; outside it, on the side they called, they do. Best of 1, 3 or 5, in English or French. Playing alone? Tap VS DOG to duel the dog, on Easy, Medium or Hard. And every day there is a Daily Question, the same for everyone. Plays fully offline.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account or internet? No. Squeeze has no accounts, no sign-in, and the game plays entirely offline. Every fact ships inside the app. Ads need a connection; without one, the game simply carries on. From version 2.1 the app also fetches a small settings file from this website at launch; offline, it uses its built-in settings.

How does the Daily Question work? Tap DAILY at the top left of the home screen. One question a day, the same for every player, and one attempt: bet your margin, place your window, inside or outside? SHARE sends a one-line result that never gives the answer away.

What are streaks and Rain Checks? Finishing the Daily Question or any match counts for the day. Every 7 days in a row earns a Rain Check (you can bank up to 3), which covers a missed day automatically. If your streak breaks anyway, you can repair it within 48 hours by watching an optional ad.

How do I turn reminders on or off? After your first Daily Question, Squeeze asks whether you want a reminder (at most one a day, never at night). Once they are allowed, the bell next to DAILY on the home screen turns them off and on; you can also turn off Squeeze’s notifications in iOS Settings.

How does “Near You” use my location? When you tap Near You, the app asks for while-using permission and matches your coordinates against a places database bundled inside the app to pick nearby trivia. Your location is processed on your phone and never transmitted anywhere. See the privacy policy.

How do I learn the rules? The first time you open Squeeze after downloading it, the fox and the dog play one round for you, without a word. To see it again, tap WATCH on the home screen (you can skip a replay).

Why do I see ads? From version 2.0, Squeeze shows an occasional ad between rounds and at the end of a match, never during a turn. Ads are non-personalized and suitable for all ages, and Squeeze never asks to track you. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the Privacy choices button on the home screen changes your ad consent. From version 2.1, buttons that start with AD play an ad only when you tap them, for a reward: repair a streak, one extra Rain Check a day, or another question change after the two free ones in a match.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app. Everything Squeeze stores (your language, mode and match length, ad counters, your streak and Daily results, the dog’s level, your reminder choice) lives only on your device.

Privacy

Squeeze itself collects nothing about you; Google AdMob receives limited device data to show ads. Read the full privacy policy.

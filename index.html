What is Squeeze?

A head-to-head estimation duel for two players sharing one iPhone or iPad. A real question appears and you claim how close your guess will land (“within 300 feet”). Your rival squeezes that claim tighter, or calls your bluff: then you place your window on the bridge, and they bet the answer is BELOW or ABOVE it. If the real answer lands inside your window, you win the round; outside it, on the side they called, they do. Best of 1, 3 or 5, in English or French. Plays fully offline.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account or internet? No. Squeeze has no accounts, no sign-in, and the game plays entirely offline. Every fact ships inside the app. Ads between rounds need a connection; without one, the game simply carries on.

How does “Near You” use my location? When you tap Near You, the app asks for while-using permission and matches your coordinates against a places database bundled inside the app to pick nearby trivia. Your location is processed on your phone and never transmitted anywhere. See the privacy policy.

How do I learn the rules? Tap WATCH on the home screen: the fox and the dog play one round for you, without a word.

Why do I see ads? From version 2.0, Squeeze shows an occasional ad between rounds and at the end of a match, never during a turn. Ads are non-personalized and suitable for all ages, and Squeeze never asks to track you. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the Privacy choices button on the home screen changes your ad consent.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app. Everything Squeeze stores (your language, mode and match length, ad counters) lives only on your device.

Privacy

Squeeze itself collects nothing about you; Google AdMob receives limited device data to show ads. Read the full privacy policy.

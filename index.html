What is Squeeze?

A head-to-head estimation duel for two players on one iPhone. A real question appears, you claim how wide a window you'd trap the answer in, take turns squeezing tighter — or force your rival to place their window and call HIGHER or LOWER. Best of 1, 3, or 5. Fully offline.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account or internet? No. Squeeze has no accounts, no sign-in, and works entirely offline. All 7,900+ facts ship inside the app.

How does “Near You” use my location? When you tap Near You, the app asks for while-using permission and matches your coordinates against a places database bundled inside the app to pick nearby trivia. Your location is processed on your phone and never transmitted anywhere. See the privacy policy.

How do I replay the tutorial? Tap “How to play” on the home screen.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app. Everything Squeeze stores (match progress, seen questions, your hero picks) lives only on your device.
